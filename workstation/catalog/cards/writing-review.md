## When

You want help with prose but need to choose what kind of help before sending it.

## Try

- **Rewrite:** ask for proposed replacement wording. Preserve the original; compare meaning, voice and facts before applying anything.
- **Feedback:** ask what is unclear and why. A critique belongs beside the text, not pasted over it.
- **Summary:** ask for a shorter account. Omissions are expected; it is not automatically an improved version of the original.

Choose a paragraph or explicit selection first. Read the scope and provider/model before sending. Selected text leaves your machine when you invoke a provider-backed AI tool; disabling local session files does not establish provider retention or training policy.

The planned writing chapter uses Fabric's `improve_writing`, `analyze_prose` and `summarize_micro` prompts through Pi. **This offline proof does not vendor prompts, run AI or provide Apply.** It teaches the review contract before that adapter is built.

Existing AI bindings have not been migrated. If you use an existing `:Llm` helper, inspect its scope: some configurations default a no-range command to the whole buffer. Do not assume a current selection or this guide's proposed review safety applies to that legacy route.

## Why

Scope and result type are decisions, not implementation details. The future review flow must reject automatic Apply after the source changes, and one explicit Apply must be undoable without saving the file.
